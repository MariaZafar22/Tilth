# Soil and Irrigation

## Soil testing
Test soil every 2 to 3 years at the nearest soil fertility lab. Collect samples from 6 to 8 spots at 0 to 15 cm depth, mix, and send about half a kilogram. Fertilizer plans from a soil test save money compared with blanket doses.

## Salinity and sodicity
White crust on the surface, poor germination and patchy growth suggest salt problems. Leach salts with good-quality water, add gypsum for sodic soils, and improve drainage. Test irrigation water before using tubewell water on sensitive crops.

## Water saving
Laser land levelling can save around 25 percent of irrigation water. Ridge or bed sowing, mulching and irrigating at critical stages also save water. Irrigate in the evening during extreme heat to reduce evaporation.

## Organic matter
Add farmyard manure or compost (about 2 to 3 trolleys per acre) and rotate with legumes like berseem or mungbean to improve soil structure and nitrogen.
