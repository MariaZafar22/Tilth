# Rice

## Nursery and transplanting
Sow the nursery between 20 May and 15 June. Seedlings are ready for transplanting after 25 to 30 days. Transplant 2 to 3 seedlings per hill with spacing of about 20 x 20 cm, between 20 June and end of July depending on variety.

## Water management
Keep 2 to 5 cm of standing water after transplanting. Alternate wetting and drying saves water: let the field dry until small cracks appear, then irrigate again. Drain the field 10 to 15 days before harvest.

## Fertilizer
Apply phosphorus and potash before transplanting. Split nitrogen into 2 to 3 doses at tillering and panicle initiation. Zinc deficiency shows as rusty brown patches on young leaves; zinc sulphate at transplanting prevents it.

## Pests and diseases
Stem borer causes dead hearts and white ears. Leaf folder rolls leaves. Bacterial leaf blight causes yellowing from leaf tips. Use clean seedlings, balanced nitrogen, and scout weekly. Spray only when the pest count crosses the economic threshold and follow the label.

## Harvest
Harvest when about 80 percent of grains in the panicle turn golden. Dry grain to about 14 percent moisture before storage.
