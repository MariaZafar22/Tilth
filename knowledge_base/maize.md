# Maize

## Sowing seasons
Spring maize is sown from 1 February to 15 March; autumn maize from 15 July to 15 August. Use hybrid seed from a trusted dealer. Seed rate is 8 to 10 kg per acre with row spacing of 75 cm and plant spacing of 20 to 22 cm.

## Irrigation
Irrigate at germination, knee-high stage, tasseling and silking, and grain filling. Tasseling and silking are the most sensitive; stress here cuts yield sharply.

## Fertilizer
Maize is a heavy feeder. Apply phosphorus and potash at sowing, and split nitrogen into 3 doses: at sowing, knee-high, and before tasseling.

## Fall armyworm
Look for ragged leaf holes and sawdust-like frass in the whorl. Scout early at seedling and whorl stages. Hand-pick egg masses in small fields, and apply registered insecticides only on label advice, directing spray into the whorl.

## Harvest
Harvest when husks dry and a black layer forms at the grain base, at about 20 to 25 percent moisture. Dry to 13 to 14 percent before storage to prevent aflatoxin.
