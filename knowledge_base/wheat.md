# Wheat

## Sowing and seed
Best sowing time in Punjab is 1 to 25 November. Every week of delay after 25 November can reduce yield. Use certified seed of an approved variety. Seed rate is about 50 kg per acre with a drill, and 60 kg per acre if sowing is late or broadcast. Treat seed against smut and seedling diseases before sowing.

## Irrigation
Wheat usually needs 4 to 5 irrigations. Critical stages are crown root initiation (about 20 to 25 days after sowing), tillering, booting or jointing, flowering and grain filling. Avoid irrigating in strong wind to reduce lodging.

## Fertilizer
Apply all DAP and potash at sowing. Give urea in two splits: half at first irrigation and half at second irrigation. Do not apply urea just before heavy rain because nitrogen is lost.

## Yellow rust
Yellow rust shows as yellow stripes of powder on leaves, worst in cool humid weather (10 to 20 C) in January and February. Scout fields weekly from December, grow resistant varieties, and report outbreaks early to the extension office. Spray a registered fungicide only if infection is confirmed, following the label.

## Weeds
Common weeds are wild oat, canary grass and broadleaf weeds like bathua. Control within 30 to 40 days after sowing with a registered herbicide suited to the weed type. Check the label for crop stage.

## Harvest
Harvest when grains are hard and straw is golden, usually in April. Late harvest causes shattering losses.
