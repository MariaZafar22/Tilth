# Pest Management Basics

## Integrated pest management
Scout fields weekly, identify the pest before acting, and use chemicals only when the pest count crosses the economic threshold. Combine resistant varieties, crop rotation, clean seed, timely sowing and natural enemies like ladybirds and lacewings.

## Safe spraying
Read the label for dose, waiting period and protective gear. Wear gloves, mask and long sleeves. Spray in calm, dry weather in the early morning or evening, never before rain. Do not mix products unless the label allows it. Triple-rinse and puncture empty containers; never reuse them for food or water.

## Resistance management
Do not repeat the same insecticide group more than twice in a season. Rotate groups as advised by the extension officer to slow resistance.

## When to ask for help
If the symptom is unclear, spreads fast, or sprays are not working, take a fresh sample to the local agriculture extension office or university lab for a proper diagnosis.
