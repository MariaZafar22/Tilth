# Sugarcane

## Planting time
Autumn planting from 15 September to 31 October gives the highest yield. Spring planting from mid February to end of March is the second choice. Use healthy, disease-free setts with 2 to 3 buds, cut from the middle of mature cane.

## Irrigation
Irrigate right after planting, then every 10 to 15 days in summer and 20 to 25 days in winter. Moisture stress during the formative stage (April to June) reduces tillers and yield.

## Fertilizer
Apply phosphorus and potash at planting. Split nitrogen into 2 to 3 doses between tillering and grand growth. Ratoon crops need extra nitrogen soon after harvest.

## Pests
Top borer and stem borer cause dead hearts. Pyrilla leafhoppers produce honeydew and black mould on leaves. Remove trash, keep field clean, and use biological control where recommended by the extension office.

## Earthing up and harvest
Earth up 2 to 3 times to prevent lodging. Harvest at full maturity as indicated by sugar content, cutting close to the ground and delivering to the mill quickly to avoid sugar loss.
