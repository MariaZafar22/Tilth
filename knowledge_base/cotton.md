# Cotton

## Sowing
Best sowing time is 1 April to 15 May; after 31 May yield drops. Use certified seed of recommended varieties. Sow on ridges or beds with plant spacing of about 25 to 30 cm and row spacing of 75 cm. Seed rate is about 8 to 10 kg per acre for delinted seed.

## Irrigation
Give the first irrigation 30 to 35 days after sowing, then every 12 to 15 days depending on soil and heat. Flowering and boll formation are the most sensitive stages; water stress causes flower and boll shedding.

## Cotton leaf curl virus
Symptoms are upward or downward curling of leaves, thickened veins and small leaf-like growths under the leaf. The virus is spread by whitefly. Use tolerant varieties, sow on time, remove weeds and alternate hosts, and monitor whitefly with yellow sticky traps. Rotate insecticide groups on label advice to avoid resistance.

## Pink bollworm
Larvae feed inside bolls and ruin lint. Early sowing, timely crop removal and destroying stalks after picking reduce carry-over. Monitor with pheromone traps from flowering and act on threshold.

## Picking
Pick when bolls open fully, every 2 to 3 weeks, in dry weather. Keep trash and green bolls out of the lot to protect quality and price.
